V85 Model LabCan a model beat the crowd at Swedish trotting?

I built a system that estimates every horse's chance of winning in ATG's V85, compares it with how the crowd bets, and looks for the races where the crowd is wrong. It is tested like a trading model, with no peeking at the future and an uncertainty range on every number.

What I found

  1. The crowd is hard to beat. A model that sees only form trails the crowd's betting percentages: its gain is −10.9 %.
  2. But it has a habit. It bets too much on favourites. Correcting that gives a small, measurable edge on V85.
  3. The backtest is optimistic, because it uses the final betting percentages. So every round from 10 October 2026 is a sealed live test, and from November each prediction is published as a hash before the race.
